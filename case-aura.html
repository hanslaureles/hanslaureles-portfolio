// 01 · OVERVIEW & ARTISANAL VISION

Balancing Craft Warmth & Checkout Velocity

Specialty coffee ordering is deeply personal: customers expect nuanced control over cup size, dairy vs. plant-based milk (oat, almond, soy), sweetness levels, and ice dilution. However, in conventional e-commerce platforms, complex variant drop-downs and multi-page checkouts trigger steep cart abandonment.

“How might we design a coffee ordering experience that feels as inviting, appetizing, and tactile as walking into your favorite neighborhood roastery?”

Aura Coffee & Kitchen solves this by pairing a warm oat-milk aesthetic (soft cream, amber caramel, pill buttons) with an ultra-fast client-side state engine: an interactive drink customizer modal, slide-over bag drawer with free delivery progress tracking, and a streamlined 1-page checkout tailored for Philippine mobile payment rails.

// 02 · USABILITY AUDITS & FRICTION POINTS

Eliminating Drop-Off in Drink Ordering

A systematic audit of standard food & beverage ordering funnels revealed three primary friction points:

1. Surprise Courier Fees at the Finish Line

Hiding delivery costs until the final payment page triggers immediate price shock. Aura introduces an active free delivery meter in the bag drawer, encouraging upsells to reach the ₱500 threshold.

2. Clunky, Inflexible Customization

Requiring customers to navigate away from the menu just to pick oat milk or adjust sweetness causes cognitive fatigue. Aura utilizes a focused, single-viewport modal with immediate visual feedback.

3. Multi-Step Checkout Exhaustion

Forcing 4 separate page transitions for Customer Info, Address, Shipping, and Payment creates friction on mobile. Aura consolidates everything onto a single, clear 1-page layout with one-click GCash and Maya buttons.

// 04 · VISUAL TRUST & LOCAL RAILS

Local Relevance & Visual Reassurance

By supporting domestic Philippine payment methods—**GCash**, **Maya**, **GrabPay**, **QR Ph**, and **Cash on Delivery (COD)**—Aura eliminates payment friction for local coffee lovers. Real-time form validation, explicit order recap cards with itemized modifications, and an instant order confirmation ticket create complete transparency and peace of mind.
