Case Study 04 Specialty Commerce Drink Customizer 6 Payment Options

Aura Coffee & Kitchen —
A Warm, One-Page Coffee Ordering Experience

A warm, welcoming coffee-shop store inspired by the mobile apps people already love. Customize your drink (size, dairy or plant milk, sweetness, ice), watch your free-delivery progress in the Shopping Bag, and check out on one page with 6 payment options (GCash, Maya, GrabPay, card, QR Ph and cash on delivery). Every order ends with a scalloped paper-style ticket.

● The Engineering Challenge

Standard e-commerce templates (Shopify/WooCommerce) suffer from heavy third-party script bloat, slow mobile load times, generic checkout flows, and rigid carts that don't support custom beverage modifiers.

● The Architecture Solution

Built a dependency-free storefront in vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript: a drink customizer, a Shopping Bag that stays saved in your browser, and 6 payment options (GCash, Maya, GrabPay, card, QR Ph and cash on delivery).

// 01 · OVERVIEW & VISION

Balancing Craft Warmth & Checkout Velocity

Specialty coffee ordering is deeply personal: customers expect nuanced control over cup size, dairy vs. plant-based milk (oat, almond, soy), sweetness levels, and ice dilution. However, in conventional e-commerce platforms, complex variant drop-downs and multi-page checkouts trigger steep cart abandonment.

“How might we design a coffee ordering experience that feels as inviting, appetizing, and tactile as walking into your favorite neighborhood roastery?”

Aura Coffee & Kitchen solves this by pairing a warm oat-milk aesthetic (soft cream, amber caramel, pill buttons) with a fast, simple ordering flow: a drink customizer, a slide-over bag drawer that tracks free delivery, and a one-page checkout built around the ways Filipinos actually pay.

// 02 · USABILITY AUDITS & FRICTION POINTS

Eliminating Drop-Off in Drink Ordering

A systematic audit of standard food & beverage ordering funnels revealed three primary friction points:

1. Surprise Courier Fees at the Finish Line

Hiding delivery costs until the final payment page triggers immediate price shock. Aura introduces an active free delivery meter in the bag drawer, encouraging upsells to reach the ₱500 threshold.

2. Clunky, Inflexible Customization

Requiring customers to navigate away from the menu just to pick oat milk or adjust sweetness causes cognitive fatigue. Aura utilizes a focused, single-viewport modal with immediate visual feedback.

3. Multi-Step Checkout Exhaustion

Forcing 4 separate page transitions for Customer Info, Address, Shipping, and Payment creates friction on mobile. Aura consolidates everything onto a single, clear 1-page layout with one-click GCash and Maya buttons.

// 04 · LOCAL PAYMENTS & REASSURANCE

Local Relevance & Visual Reassurance

With 6 payment options (GCash, Maya, GrabPay, card, QR Ph and cash on delivery), local coffee lovers can pay the way they already do. Real-time form validation, explicit order recap cards with itemized modifications, and an instant order confirmation ticket create complete transparency and peace of mind.
