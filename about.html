The Engineer Behind the Architecture

I build AI agents that are useful,
and interfaces people enjoy using.

I'm Hans Aaron Laureles, a Computer Science graduate from De La Salle University–Dasmariñas focused on AI agent teams that use real tools, running models locally or in the cloud, and the full-stack web apps around them.

I studied distributed computing, databases and algorithms. When language models started using real tools instead of only generating text, I moved my focus there: routing work across several models, running them on my own GPU, and making tool use predictable.

My frontend and UI/UX work, sharpened during my internship at ROC.PH, shapes how I build agents: I treat them as products people use. The agents are hosted on my own PC, and the screens around them should be quick to read and hard to misuse.
