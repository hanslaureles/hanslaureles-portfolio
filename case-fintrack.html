// 01 · OVERVIEW & PROBLEM

The Intimidation of Modern Budgeting

Traditional banking applications are optimized for accounting compliance rather than human comprehension. Young professionals entering the workforce frequently abandon budgeting apps within two weeks because logging expenses requires manual categorical dropdowns, complex math calculations, and dense tabular screens.

“How can we transform dense financial tracking into a frictionless daily habit that takes less than five seconds to complete?”

FinTrack was engineered from the ground up to replace numeric intimidation with visual proportion: displaying spending health through clear color-coded budget rings, instant one-tap expense logging, and category breakdowns.

// 02 · USER RESEARCH & INSIGHTS

Key Usability Friction Factors

Through user testing sessions with entry-level professionals, three distinct behavioral hurdles emerged:

1. Manual Input Friction

Every additional tap required to log an expense (e.g. selecting 4 nested categories) reduced daily app engagement by over 40%.

2. Lack of Contextual Safe-to-Spend Limits

Users care less about historical ledger history and more about a single critical question: "How much can I safely spend today?"

3. Cluttered Financial Visualizations

Tiny line graphs on small mobile screens caused perceptual confusion rather than actionable insight.
