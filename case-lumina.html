// 01 · OVERVIEW & CHALLENGE

Taming Data Fragmentation & Observability Silos

Modern engineering teams toggle between 6–10 disconnected tools (Jira, Linear, Datadog, Slack, Prometheus) to understand system and sprint throughput. This fragmented workflow introduces severe context-switching penalties and obscures operational bottlenecks.

“How can we present high-density data visualizations without overwhelming the user, maintaining sub-100ms perceptual responsiveness across all viewport sizes?”

Lumina was built to synthesize project telemetry, sprint velocity burnups, and deep-work performance metrics into a modular, single-pane command center powered by a cohesive tokenized design system.

// 02 · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Three-Tier Modular Hierarchy

To prevent information overload, Lumina separates team data into three progressive disclosure levels:

1. The Glanceable Executive Strip

Key performance indicators (Sprint Progress, Active Burnup, Deep Work Ratio) visible in constant 120px header real estate.

2. The Dynamic Focus Workspace

Kanban and task prioritization grids equipped with multi-column filtering and instant keyboard navigability.

3. Telemetry & Deep Insights

Granular time tracking, burn-down distributions, and team velocity trends with custom interactive charts.
